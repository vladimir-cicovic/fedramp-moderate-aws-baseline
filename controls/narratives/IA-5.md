# IA-5 Authenticator Management

| Family | Baseline | Responsibility | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identification and Authentication | Moderate | Customer | Implemented |

Enhancements addressed: IA-5(1) password-based authentication, IA-5(2)
public key based authentication (OIDC/TLS), IA-5(6) protection of
authenticators, IA-5(7) no embedded unencrypted static authenticators.

## Control summary

Manage system authenticators: verify identity before issuance, establish
initial content, ensure strength, distribute and revoke securely, change
defaults, change or refresh on a schedule or on compromise, protect them, and
do not embed them in applications.

## FedRAMP Rev 5 parameters

- IA-5(1): passwords at least 14 characters (FedRAMP Moderate commonly adopts the Security Hub / CIS value); complexity; 24 generations remembered; the Rev 5 baseline follows NIST 800-63B and does not require periodic rotation, only on evidence of compromise. This baseline keeps a 60-day maximum age as a conservative choice.
- Refresh of other authenticators: KMS keys yearly; database master password rotated by RDS; CI tokens per job.

## Implementation

**Passwords (IA-5(1)).** The account password policy requires 14 characters,
upper, lower, digit and symbol, prevents reuse of the last 24, and expires
passwords after 60 days. It applies only to IAM users, which are a transitional
exception; humans authenticate through Identity Center, whose password policy
is set in the identity account (and in the corporate IdP in production).

**Access keys (IA-5(7)).** No access keys are issued for CI or workloads.
GitHub Actions presents an OIDC JWT; STS validates the issuer, `aud` and
`sub` claims against the role trust policy and issues one-hour credentials.
The containment Lambda and every AWS service use IAM roles. The conformance
pack rule `AccessKeysRotated` (90 days) and the unused-credentials rule (35
days) catch any key that does appear.

**Database credentials.** Aurora's master password is created, stored and
rotated by RDS in Secrets Manager (`manage_master_user_password`), encrypted
with the customer managed data key. It never appears in code, Terraform state
or variables. Applications use IAM database authentication tokens (15-minute
lifetime) or fetch the secret over the `secretsmanager-fips` endpoint.

**Keys (IA-5, SC-12).** All three KMS customer managed keys rotate
automatically every 365 days; the previous key material stays available for
decryption; key policies restrict use to specific services under conditions.

**Protection (IA-5(6)).** Secrets Manager and KMS are reachable from the VPC
only through interface endpoints; Lambda environment variables are encrypted
with the logging key; the SNS topic and SQS queue are encrypted.

**Defaults (IA-5 e).** Terraform never sets a default password; Aurora's
master credential is generated by RDS at creation.

## Terraform

- `modules/identity/main.tf` (password policy, OIDC)
- `modules/data/main.tf` (`manage_master_user_password`, `master_user_secret_kms_key_id`)
- `modules/logging/kms.tf`, `modules/crypto/main.tf` (rotation)
- `modules/detection/config_conformance.tf` (parameters)

## Gaps and POA&M

- Bootstrap IAM users hold access keys; delete after Identity Center onboarding.
- Identity Center password and MFA policy live in the identity account and are not Terraform-managed; capture screenshots as evidence.
